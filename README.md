## Project 2 Part 2 Web Scraped Data Analysis and Visualizations

Purpose: To explore the first five Hacker News pages with built-in pandas functions, 
perform data preprocessing/cleaning as well as basic EDA with points, domains and comments

## 4 DS Questions Asked & Answered

1. What is the relationship between a story popularity versus the points?
2. What is the relationship between a story popularity versus the comments?
3. Can we distinguish if a story appears on the front page based on a higher amount of points and comments? Versus
   if a story appears in the further pages based on a lower amount of points and comments vice versa?
4.  Which website domain is among the most popular across all five pages? Can we draw a relationship between
   website domain popularity against points and comments?
